Frequently Asked Questions

What time should I arrive at the ceremony? ▾

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the 10:00 AM ceremony at Saint Vincent so everyone can be comfortably seated before the entourage begins.

Can I bring a plus one? ▾

Due to venue capacity at Gestdan Centrum Bokawkan, we can only accommodate the guests formally listed on your invitation. Thank you for understanding!

Are children allowed at the wedding? ▾

While we love your little ones, our celebration is an adults-only event with the exception of children in our immediate family and entourage.

Is parking available at the venues? ▾

Yes, dedicated guest parking is available at both Saint Vincent and Gestdan Centrum Bokawkan.
